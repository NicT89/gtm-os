## Why this passes the falsifiability test

Sent to a different company unchanged, it breaks in five places: the named stack
(Segment, dbt, Apollo), the 40% figure quoted from the prospect's own post, the
12-day-old RevOps Lead posting, the Series A stage, and the compliance-buyer path
that is specific to their market. Each is traceable to a gate-verified input field.

## Failure modes this avoided

- **No flattery, no company adjectives.** No "exciting growth," no "impressive
  traction."
- **No invented figures.** The 40% is the prospect's own number, quoted from a
  captured post — not an estimate of their funnel.
- **No pricing.** The blueprint anchors against the cost of the unfilled role, and
  retainer pricing never appears in cold outreach.
- **No claim of a custom video.** If the sequence carries a general video, the email
  frames it as "why I work this way" and the personalization lives here, in text.
