# Free Tool Case Benchmarks

Real free tools and the pattern each one proves. Upstream listed results figures for each without sources; they are removed under this plugin's no-invented-numbers rule. Before quoting a figure for any of these to a client, find the company's own published source and link it.

## Framing: "Your product is my marketing opportunity"

Bezos's line was **"your margin is my opportunity"** — where a competitor monetizes something, undercut it. The engineering-as-marketing version is **"your product is my marketing opportunity."** Take a capability others sell, build a simple free version, and turn it into an acquisition channel. Unsplash gave away the stock photos that Getty charged for — and Getty ended up acquiring it.

## Case Library

| Tool | Company | Pattern |
|------|---------|---------|
| Unsplash | Crew | Built cheaply from leftover assets; later acquired by Getty |
| Website Grader | HubSpot | Small build; grader that doubles as lead engine and qualifier |
| Forecasting | Baremetrics | Adjacent to the core product; converts trials |
| Headline Analyzer | CoSchedule | Repeat-use analyzer with a path to subscription |
| Keyword Explorer | Moz | A free surface of the paid product itself |
| Salary Calculator | Buffer | Not adjacent; pure reach and brand |
| Hatchful | Shopify | Generator that feeds core onboarding |

## What each case teaches

- **Crew → Unsplash** — The anchor case. A near-zero-cost byproduct (leftover photos from a redesign, ~3 hrs to ship) became a top-of-funnel giant. Give away what others charge for; the reach compounds.
- **HubSpot Website Grader** — Small build, large return. Proof that an analyzer/grader can double as a lead engine *and* a qualification engine — most leads auto-qualified because the tool's inputs revealed fit.
- **Baremetrics Forecasting** — A tool adjacent to the core product (revenue analytics) that converts trials well because using it makes the paid product's value obvious.
- **CoSchedule Headline Analyzer** — Repeat-use analyzer with a low-friction path to subscription. High recurring usage keeps the brand in front of the audience.
- **Moz Keyword Explorer** — A free surface of the paid product itself: it converts trials and lowers CAC because the tool *is* the demo.
- **Buffer Salary Calculator** — Not adjacent to the product at all, but massively shareable. Pure reach + brand play that still converts.
- **Shopify Hatchful** — A generator (logo maker) that feeds the core product's onboarding, converting trials.

## How to use these benchmarks

- **Set expectations**: Analyzer/grader tools tend to convert visitors to leads well and qualify them; calculators skew toward reach and share-worthiness; generators feed onboarding.
- **Justify the build**: Estimate your expected lead value × volume from your own funnel data before committing engineering time.
- **Pattern-match**: Find the case closest to your concept (adjacent-to-product vs pure-reach) and borrow its gating and distribution approach.
