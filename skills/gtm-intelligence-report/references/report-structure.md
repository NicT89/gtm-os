# Default report structure

Cover (client, kicker, title, three stat tiles that are counts of things in the database, project ID, version, status, date, confidentiality line). Then contents.

| # | Section | Must contain |
|---|---|---|
| | Document control | project ID, version, status, date, how to read labels, evidence ID legend, method and time window |
| 1 | Executive summary | five findings, each label-first, each with evidence IDs; one callout for the single decision needed |
| 2 | The company and what the calls established | team, customers, stack and history as stated (UNVERIFIED unless a public source confirms); quotes are person-neutral |
| 3 | Customer segments and the reverse ICP | stated segments as hypotheses; reverse-ICP method and rubric; result table once scored; interview plan (5 to 10 paying customers in the first 30 days) |
| 4 | Competitive landscape | direct and adjacent set, capability matrix, entry-price comparison, how the target market differs from each; every figure with an evidence ID |
| 5 | Pricing and packaging | history observed with dates and archive evidence; what changed (price, features, plans); customers before and after the change as separate cohorts; position against the market |
| 6 | Product fit: five gates | Pull, Prove, Pay, Use, Keep with metrics and thresholds (TBD until agreed); buyer paths as discovered, or `unknown`; premium-tier motion hypothesis; messaging guardrail |
| 7 | Recommendations | two categories, kept separate: (a) strategic recommendations, (b) next steps |
| 8 | 30/60/90 day plan | one table: days 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90; a named deliverable and an exit condition per line |
| 9 | Tooling and terms | what the customer receives and the commercial terms exactly as stated in the current SOW; anything not agreed is `TBD`; value estimates are labelled estimates |
| A to F | Appendices | evidence web, evidence internal, claim ledger, competitor reports, open questions, version history and corrections |

## Notes

- The five findings should each have one KNOWN, and where relevant one INFERRED, line so the reader can see which is which.
- Every competitor report created or used gets an appendix D entry: location, version, scope, key sources, caveats, and its source URLs with capture status.
- Open questions in appendix E are refreshed from the database each version; answered ones stay, with date and source.
- Corrections to an earlier version are listed in appendix F with the reason and evidence.
